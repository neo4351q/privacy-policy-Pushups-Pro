# privacy-policy-Pushups-Pro

Application: Pushups
Last updated: March 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our mobile application "Pushups" (hereinafter — the "App") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect
To provide the App's full functionality, synchronize your results, and improve service quality, we may collect the following data:
Usage and achievement data: The App collects information about your workouts, including the number of push-ups completed, your progress, and unlocked achievements. This data is transmitted to and stored on remote servers using third-party cloud services (specifically Firebase).
Device identifiers (Device ID): We collect unique identifiers of your device to correctly associate your progress with your account and for analytics purposes.
Technical data: The App collects information about your hardware (device model), operating system (Android version), and the version of the installed App.

2. How We Use Your Data
The collected information is used solely for:
Providing the core functionality of the App, including saving and synchronizing your workout history and achievements.
Improving App stability, analyzing errors and crashes.
Analytics of user interactions with the App (e.g., understanding which features are most popular).

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs
The App uses third-party services that may collect and process information about you and your device:
Firebase / Google Play Services: Used for cloud storage and synchronization of your achievements, as well as for analytics and crash reporting (Crashlytics).
RuStore SDK / NashStore SDK: Used for app store internal mechanics (e.g., update checks, in-app purchases if applicable).
Please refer to the respective official websites to review the privacy policies of these services.

4. Your Rights
In accordance with applicable law, you have the right to:
Request information about what data we store about you.
Request the complete deletion of your data (including workout history and achievements) from our servers. To do so, please contact us using the details below.
Uninstall the App from your device or restrict access to certain device features via Android system settings.

5. Security
We take all reasonable and technically feasible measures to protect collected data from unauthorized access. Third-party services (such as Firebase) employ industry-standard encryption when transmitting and storing user data.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added or as our data processing practices change. Important changes take effect upon publication in the App or on this policy page.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, or if you wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact us:
